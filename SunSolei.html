SunSolei Soothing Suncare

Originally an organic sunscreen packaging project from my Intro to Graphic Design class, I revisited and expanded it by developing a comprehensive brand identity as a case study. This included refining the packaging design and exploring various branding elements such as logo, color palette, and typography to effectively cater to a specific target demographic (sucess, rich, educated, classy, and organic mom).
